Good to know

What do I need to rent a car?

A driving licence held for at least two years, your ID card or passport, and a deposit. Drivers must be 23 or older.

Is insurance included?

Yes. Every rental includes full insurance. You can reduce the deductible to zero for 15 DT per day.

Can I return the car in another city?

Yes, between any of our pick-up points. A one-way fee applies depending on the distance.

What happens if I return the car late?

You have a free 1-hour grace period. After that, an extra day is charged. Message us if you're running late and we'll sort it out.
